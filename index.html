Photo Deleter: Clear Space

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy explains how Photo Deleter: Clear Space (also called Photo Deleter, “the app,” “we,” or “us”) handles information on iOS and Android. The app is provided by Paule Cyrusse See. You can contact us at photodeleterapp@gmail.com.

At a glance
  • Your photos and recognized text are analyzed on your device. We do not upload them to our servers or supply them to advertisers.
  • No app account is required. You choose which photos the app can access and which photos to delete.
  • The app uses Google AdMob for advertising and requests non-personalized, G-rated ads with child-directed protections for every user.
  • Advertising and Android text-recognition services can process technical and diagnostic information, as explained below.

1. Photos and photo-library access

With your device permission, Photo Deleter reads accessible photos, thumbnails, and related library information to suggest duplicate or similar photos, likely blur, screenshots, temporary reference photos, saved images, and possible exposure problems. Library information can include photo identifiers, creation or modification times, dimensions, screenshot indicators, and available filenames or source-folder information.

Image comparisons, blur and exposure checks, and text recognition run on your device. Recognized text is used temporarily for classification and is not saved as a transcript. Photos, thumbnails, and recognized text are not sent by the app to the developer, AdMob, or a cloud image-analysis service.

If a photo is stored in iCloud or another device-managed photo service, your operating system or photo provider may download the photo or its thumbnail under your existing settings. Those services have their own privacy practices.

You may grant limited access where supported, change the permitted photos, or revoke access in your device settings. The app reviews only the photos made accessible to it. Deleting a photo requires your explicit selection and confirmation, followed by the operating system’s deletion process. Recovery or permanent deletion depends on your device and operating-system version.

2. Information saved on your device

The app stores scan results and progress, photo identifiers and selected metadata, similarity groups, photo-category suggestions, analysis measurements or signatures, photos marked to keep, and basic app preferences. This allows you to resume review and avoid repeating completed analysis.

This information is stored in the app’s local storage. It is not a developer-hosted photo backup. Starting another scan replaces the saved scan results; analysis caches and keep preferences may remain for later scans. Device backup and restore settings may include app storage.

You can remove local app data using your device’s app-storage controls where available or by deleting the app. Operating-system backups may retain copies according to your backup settings. Deleting app data does not itself delete photos from your photo library.

3. Advertising and privacy choices

Photo Deleter displays banner ads provided by Google AdMob. We apply the same child-directed ad treatment to all users, request only non-personalized ads, and set the maximum ad content rating to G. We do not collect your birth date or use an age-screening profile. These settings ask Google to disable personalized advertising and remarketing, apply child-serving protections, and avoid transmitting the Android advertising ID or Apple advertising identifier for these requests.

Non-personalized advertising still uses information to deliver and measure ads, limit repeat displays, maintain security, and prevent fraud. Depending on the platform and applicable restrictions, Google may process IP addresses and approximate location derived from them, app and device information, ad impressions and interactions, diagnostic and performance information, and permitted app or installation identifiers. We do not send Google your photo-library contents or recognized text.

Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) manages applicable privacy signals and choices. The app applies under-age-of-consent treatment for all users in line with its child-directed ad configuration, so a personalized-ad consent form may not be shown. If Google requires a privacy-options entry point, the app displays Ad privacy options so you can review or change the available choices. Changing a choice affects subsequent requests as supported by the service.

For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Google’s age-restricted ad treatment. You can also use the advertising privacy controls in your device settings.

4. Text recognition and technical information

On iOS, text recognition uses Apple Vision on the device. On Android, it uses Google ML Kit with a bundled text-recognition model. Google states that ML Kit does not send input images, recognized text, or recognition results to its servers.

ML Kit can contact Google for service maintenance and process technical metrics such as device model and operating-system version, app version, per-installation identifiers, API configuration, processing times, feature events, and errors. These metrics support diagnostics, maintenance, usage measurement, and abuse prevention. See ML Kit’s privacy information and ML Kit’s Android data disclosures.

The app does not require sign-in and does not operate a developer-run account, photo-upload, or analytics server. Third-party SDK technical processing described in this policy is separate from the on-device handling of your photos.

5. Children and families

Photo Deleter may be used by people of different ages. We use child-directed ad protections for everyone rather than collecting ages to decide who receives those protections. The app does not ask children for a name, email address, birth date, precise location, or account, and does not upload their photos or recognized text.

The ad and technical-service processing described above can still take place. We do not use children’s photo contents to build advertising profiles or request behaviorally personalized ads. A parent or guardian can control photo access, remove app data, or contact us about a child’s privacy at photodeleterapp@gmail.com. Please do not email personal photos or other sensitive information unless it is necessary to resolve your request.

6. When you contact us

If you email us, we receive the address you use and the information you choose to include. We use that correspondence to respond, provide support, investigate reported problems, and handle privacy requests. We retain correspondence only as reasonably needed for those purposes or applicable legal obligations. Our email provider also processes correspondence under its own terms.

7. Sharing, retention, and security

We do not sell your photos or provide them to advertising services. Google processes the advertising and ML Kit information described above according to the applicable service configuration and its privacy policies. Their retention periods and processing locations are governed by those services; we do not promise a fixed deletion period for information held by Google.

We may disclose information you provide directly to us when reasonably necessary to comply with a legal obligation, respond to a valid legal process, or protect rights and security. Service providers may process information in countries other than your own.

We use the app sandbox and operating-system permissions to limit access to local app data. Google documents encryption in transit for its ad and ML Kit technical data. No device or method of transmission is completely secure.

8. Your controls and privacy requests

You can change photo permissions, manage device advertising settings, use the app’s privacy-options control when available, and remove local app data. Because we do not receive your photos or local scan database, we cannot retrieve or delete those local items remotely.

Depending on where you live and the information involved, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or an objection to processing, and to withdraw consent where processing relies on it. You may also have the right to raise a concern with your local privacy regulator. Contact photodeleterapp@gmail.com for requests concerning information we hold. We may need enough information to verify and respond to your request. For information held by Google, its privacy tools and request processes may also be relevant.

9. This policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no advertising, analytics scripts, contact forms, or tracking pixels added by us. GitHub processes technical request information, including visitor IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following a link to another service takes you to that service’s privacy practices.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The current version and its effective date will appear on this page. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable rules.
